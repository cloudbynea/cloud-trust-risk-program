# Quarterly Cloud Health Review  
## Cloud Trust & Risk Program  
**Cybersecurity SaaS | AWS Environment**

---

## Purpose of This Review

This Quarterly Cloud Health Review (QCHR) is the primary executive-facing artifact of the Cloud Trust & Risk Program.

Its purpose is to provide leadership with a structured, outcome-driven view of:

- cloud trust posture  
- identity and access risk  
- financial exposure  
- incident learnings  
- and governance effectiveness  

This review reframes cloud from an operational system into a **business control system.**

---

## Executive Outcomes

Each quarterly review is designed to answer five leadership questions:

1. Are we safe to scale?  
2. Do we control access to our platform?  
3. Can we trust our cloud financial signals?  
4. Are risks increasing or decreasing?  
5. What decisions does leadership need to make?

---

## Review Structure

---

# 1. Executive Summary

### Quarter in Review

High-level summary of:

- overall cloud trust posture  
- material changes since last review  
- executive-relevant developments  

### Leadership Snapshot

- overall risk trajectory (improving / stable / deteriorating)  
- top achievements  
- top unresolved risks  

### Executive Attention Areas

Clear articulation of:

- decisions required  
- investments needed  
- policy enforcement gaps  
- upcoming trust constraints  

---

# 2. Business & Platform Context Update

- major business changes  
- new enterprise customers  
- regulatory or audit developments  
- architectural shifts  
- operating model changes  

Purpose: ensure cloud risk is interpreted through **current business reality.**

---

# 3. Cloud Risk Heatmap

Updated executive heatmap including:

- root and MFA governance  
- privileged access posture  
- orphaned identities  
- policy hygiene  
- incident readiness  
- billing and financial trust signals  

For each domain:

- current severity  
- trend vs last quarter  
- business interpretation  
- leadership implication  

This section anchors **prioritization.**

---

# 4. Identity & Access Posture Review

Executive view of:

- privileged access exposure  
- MFA and root protection status  
- access ownership coverage  
- offboarding effectiveness  
- certification results  

Includes:

- structural improvements  
- unresolved governance gaps  
- enforcement effectiveness  

Outcome: clarity on **who controls access and how well.**

---

# 5. Security & Incident Insights

Summary of:

- identity-related incidents  
- near-miss patterns  
- systemic failure points  
- response effectiveness  
- recovery velocity  

Includes:

- what changed structurally  
- what risks were reduced  
- what patterns remain  

Outcome: demonstrate whether incidents are **strengthening or weakening** the organization.

---

# 6. Cloud Financial Health & Billing Trust

Executive financial trust view:

- cost trend overview  
- anomaly patterns  
- orphaned spend signals  
- ownership coverage  
- optimization posture  

Includes:

- financial exposure risks  
- cost governance maturity  
- confidence in billing accuracy  
- enterprise contract readiness  

Outcome: establish whether leadership can **trust cloud financial signals.**

---

# 7. Governance & Operating Model Health

Assessment of:

- identity ownership performance  
- steering committee effectiveness  
- enforcement adherence  
- incident operating rhythm maturity  
- audit posture alignment  

Includes:

- unresolved accountability gaps  
- policy adherence issues  
- decision bottlenecks  

Outcome: measure **leadership control maturity.**

---

# 8. Strategic Risk Register

Consolidated view of:

- top enterprise risks  
- cross-domain dependencies  
- accepted risks  
- emerging exposure areas  

Each entry includes:

- business impact  
- likelihood  
- owner  
- current treatment  
- leadership disposition  

Outcome: formalize risk as a **managed executive asset.**

---

# 9. 90-Day Forward Cloud Roadmap

Phased plan across:

- identity and access  
- security posture  
- financial governance  
- operating model maturity  

Includes:

- structural initiatives  
- remediation streams  
- executive actions  
- success indicators  

Outcome: align teams and leadership around **clear forward motion.**

---

# 10. Executive Decisions & Commitments

Explicit capture of:

- decisions made  
- policies approved  
- risks accepted  
- investments authorized  
- ownership assignments  

This section becomes the **contract** between leadership and the cloud program.

---

# 11. Appendices (As Needed)

- supporting dashboards  
- technical deep dives  
- audit mapping  
- RCA summaries  
- metric definitions  

---

## Relationship to Cloud Trust & Risk Program

This review consolidates inputs from:

- Executive Cloud Health Dashboard  
- Cloud Risk Heatmap  
- Identity Ownership Framework  
- Post-Incident Operating Rhythm  
- Cloud Leadership Roadmap  

It functions as the central executive mechanism for cloud governance.

---

## Role of the TAM / Executive Cloud Advisor

The TAM owns:

- review design  
- executive translation  
- risk framing  
- cross-functional alignment  
- leadership facilitation  
- longitudinal tracking  

The TAM ensures the organization does not simply operate cloud.

It ensures leadership **governs cloud.**

---

## Strategic Outcome

The Quarterly Cloud Health Review institutionalizes cloud trust.

It ensures:

- risks are visible  
- decisions are recorded  
- ownership is enforced  
- and growth is not built on hidden exposure.
