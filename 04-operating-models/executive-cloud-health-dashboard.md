# Executive Cloud Health Dashboard

### Cloud Trust & Risk Program

**Cybersecurity SaaS | AWS Environment**

---

## Purpose

The Executive Cloud Health Dashboard provides leadership with a consolidated, decision-oriented view of cloud trust posture across security, access, and financial exposure.

It is designed to support:

• executive decision-making
• risk prioritization
• governance accountability
• and quarterly planning

---

## Design Philosophy

This dashboard does not present cloud metrics.

It presents **business-relevant signals.**

Every indicator must answer one of four questions:

• Are we safe to scale?
• Are we controlling access?
• Can we trust our spend?
• Are risks trending up or down?

---

## Core Dashboard Domains

### 1. Identity & Access Risk

• privileged identity count
• MFA compliance coverage
• orphaned identity detection
• access certification completion
• root account protection status

Outcome signal: administrative control health.

---

### 2. Security Posture & Incident Signals

• identity-related incidents
• containment time
• near-miss frequency
• repeat failure patterns
• unresolved control gaps

Outcome signal: trust resilience.

---

### 3. Financial Exposure & Billing Trust

• unexplained cost variance
• orphaned resource spend
• service ownership coverage
• chargeback accuracy
• optimization pipeline status

Outcome signal: cloud financial governability.

---

### 4. Governance & Accountability

• ownership assignments
• policy compliance rates
• remediation aging
• executive risk acceptances
• audit readiness posture

Outcome signal: leadership control maturity.

---

## Executive Views

### Monthly Executive Summary

• top 3 risk movements

• remediation progress

### Quarterly Cloud Health Review

• heatmap refresh
• trend analysis
• roadmap adjustments
• strategic investment signals

---

## Operational Integration

The dashboard aggregates data from:

• IAM and identity tooling
• security monitoring
• incident management
• billing and cost platforms
• governance workflows

It is maintained as a **single source of truth** for leadership.

---

## Strategic Value

This dashboard enables leadership to:

• detect risk before incidents
• allocate remediation resources
• validate cloud readiness for enterprise growth
• demonstrate control to auditors and customers

It reframes cloud from an engineering system into a **business operating system.**
