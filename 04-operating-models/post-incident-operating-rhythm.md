# Post-Incident Operating Rhythm  
## Cloud Trust & Risk Program  
**Cybersecurity SaaS | AWS Environment**

---

## Purpose

This operating rhythm defines how the organization responds, stabilizes, learns, and structurally improves after identity-, access-, or cloud-trust-related incidents and near-miss events.

Its objective is not incident closure.

Its objective is to ensure every incident strengthens executive control, reduces systemic risk, and improves organizational resilience.

---

## Executive Principle

Incidents are not technical failures.

They are **governance signals.**

Every incident reveals where authority, ownership, enforcement, or visibility has broken down.

This operating rhythm ensures incidents are converted into:

- structural improvements  
- governance corrections  
- leadership decisions  
- and measurable trust gains  

---

## Incident Classification Scope

This operating rhythm applies to:

- identity and access incidents  
- root or MFA control failures  
- privileged access misuse  
- former employee access exposure  
- billing or financial trust incidents  
- cloud security near-misses  

---

## Phase 1 — Immediate Stabilization (0–24 Hours)

**Strategic Objective:**  
Contain risk, preserve trust, and establish executive clarity.

### Core Actions

- Access containment and isolation  
- Credential invalidation and rotation  
- Forensic data preservation  
- Initial blast-radius assessment  
- Customer and stakeholder communication alignment  

### Leadership Outputs

- Incident executive brief (what happened, current state, immediate risk)  
- Containment confirmation  
- Decision log initiation  

### Ownership

- Technical containment: Security / IAM  
- Business coordination: TAM / Cloud Advisor  
- Executive awareness: CTO / Security leadership  

Outcome: the organization regains operational control.

---

## Phase 2 — Executive RCA & Governance Review (24–72 Hours)

**Strategic Objective:**  
Convert the incident from an event into an organizational insight.

### Mandatory Executive RCA

A standardized, blame-free Root Cause Analysis session is conducted within 48–72 hours.

Focus areas:

- failure pathways  
- control breakdowns  
- ownership gaps  
- enforcement weaknesses  
- detection and response latency  

This RCA does not ask:

“Who made the mistake?”

It asks:

“Why did the system allow this to be possible?”

### RCA Deliverables

- verified incident timeline  
- contributing systemic factors  
- impacted control domains  
- decision points and missed signals  
- preliminary structural recommendations  

### Leadership Outputs

- Executive RCA summary  
- Governance gap register  
- Immediate control corrections  
- Dashboard risk updates  

Outcome: the incident becomes a governance data point.

---

## Phase 3 — Structural Remediation & Trust Reinforcement (7–30 Days)

**Strategic Objective:**  
Ensure the incident permanently reduces organizational risk.

### Structural Actions

- policy or ownership updates  
- control redesign  
- access model corrections  
- monitoring or enforcement expansion  
- executive approval of remediation roadmap  

### Program Integration

- risk heatmap updated  
- dashboard indicators recalibrated  
- leadership roadmap adjusted  
- ownership accountability confirmed  

### Executive Outputs

- structural remediation plan  
- risk acceptance or closure statements  
- customer and audit positioning updates  
- operational playbook refinements  

Outcome: the organization exits the incident stronger than it entered.

---

## Executive Communication Model

Each incident produces three executive-level artifacts:

1. **Immediate executive brief**  
   What happened, current exposure, decisions required.

2. **Executive RCA summary**  
   Systemic causes, governance implications, leadership actions.

3. **Structural remediation plan**  
   What permanently changes as a result.

These artifacts feed the Executive Cloud Health Dashboard and Quarterly Cloud Health Reviews.

---

## Relationship to Governance Frameworks

This operating rhythm is structurally linked to:

- Identity Ownership Framework  
- Executive Cloud Health Dashboard  
- Quarterly Cloud Health Reviews  
- Cloud Leadership Roadmap  

Incidents directly influence:

- executive risk prioritization  
- governance model evolution  
- financial exposure evaluation  
- enterprise trust posture  

---

## Success Indicators

- decreasing recurrence of identity incidents  
- faster containment time  
- shrinking blast radius  
- reduced orphaned access exposure  
- improved audit posture  
- increased executive visibility  

---

## Strategic Outcome

This operating rhythm ensures the organization does not recover from incidents.

It **evolves through them.**

Incidents become a managed input into leadership decision-making, not an interruption to operations.

---

## Role of the TAM / Executive Cloud Advisor

The TAM function owns:

- executive translation  
- RCA facilitation  
- governance mapping  
- cross-functional alignment  
- structural follow-through  

The TAM ensures incidents strengthen the system rather than exhaust it.
