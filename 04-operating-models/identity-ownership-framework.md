# Identity Ownership Framework

### Cloud Trust & Risk Program

**Cybersecurity SaaS | AWS Environment**

---

## Purpose

This framework establishes formal ownership, accountability, and operating processes for cloud identity and access governance.

Its objective is to transition identity from a technical function into a **business-owned control system** aligned with enterprise risk management, audit readiness, and operational continuity.

---

## Executive Principle

Access is not a permission problem.

Access is a **business authority problem.**

Every identity must have:
• a business owner
• a documented justification
• an enforced lifecycle
• an audit trail

---

## Identity Ownership Model

Cloud identity accountability is distributed across three layers:

### 1. Executive Ownership

**Accountable Roles:**
CTO, Head of Engineering, Head of Security

**Responsibilities:**
• approve identity governance policy
• own root and MFA standards
• sponsor enforcement authority
• resolve cross-functional conflicts
• review quarterly risk posture

Executive leadership owns **identity outcomes.**

---

### 2. Business Identity Owners

**Accountable Roles:**
Department Heads (Engineering, Security, Finance, Support, etc.)

**Responsibilities:**
• approve access requests for their teams
• validate quarterly access certifications
• ensure offboarding execution
• accept or remediate risk findings
• assign functional access stewards

Business leaders own **who has access and why.**

---

### 3. Cloud Identity Stewards

**Accountable Roles:**
Security Engineering, IAM Operations

**Responsibilities:**
• implement access controls
• enforce policy baselines
• operate lifecycle workflows
• maintain identity tooling
• produce audit evidence

Cloud teams own **how access is technically implemented.**

---

## Access Lifecycle Governance

### Onboarding

• access requests tied to role definitions
• business owner approval required
• privilege level classification applied
• MFA and security baselines enforced

Outcome: no unowned identities enter the environment.

---

### Modification

• access change requests routed through business owners
• privilege escalations reviewed centrally
• temporary access time-bound
• change logging mandatory

Outcome: access evolves intentionally.

---

### Offboarding

• employment-triggered revocation
• automated identity disabling
• credential and key invalidation
• post-offboarding verification

Outcome: orphaned identities are structurally eliminated.

---

### Periodic Certification

• quarterly access reviews by department
• privilege recertification
• anomaly remediation
• executive reporting

Outcome: identity posture remains continuously accurate.

---

## Privileged Access Governance

Privileged access operates under enhanced control conditions:

• executive-approved eligibility
• role-based authorization
• time-bound elevation
• activity logging
• quarterly blast-radius review

Privileged access is treated as a **temporary business trust**, not a standing convenience.

---

## Enforcement Mechanisms

Governance is ineffective without enforcement.

This framework mandates:

• automated compliance validation
• non-compliance escalation paths
• executive exception ownership
• systemic throttling of violations

The TAM function provides visibility.

Leadership provides authority.

---

## Audit & Compliance Alignment

This framework supports:

• SOC 2 / ISO 27001 controls
• least-privilege enforcement
• access lifecycle evidence
• privileged identity audit trails

It enables consistent production of:

• ownership records
• access approvals
• revocation logs
• certification reports

---

## Executive Reporting

Identity ownership performance is reported quarterly, including:

• privileged identity counts
• orphaned identity detection
• MFA compliance rates
• certification completion
• outstanding risk acceptances

These indicators feed the Executive Cloud Health Dashboard.

---

## Strategic Outcome

This framework transforms identity from a technical dependency into an **organizational control system.**

It enables:

• scalable growth
• audit readiness
• reduced breach probability
• faster incident containment
• executive accountability
