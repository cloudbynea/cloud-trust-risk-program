# Cloud Risk Heatmap  
## Quarterly Cloud Health Review  
**Cybersecurity SaaS | AWS Environment**

---

## Purpose

This heatmap provides an executive-level view of the most material cloud risks impacting platform resilience, enterprise trust, audit readiness, and revenue protection.

Risks are evaluated by:

- Business impact  
- Recoverability  
- Likelihood  

---

## Executive Heatmap Overview

| Risk Domain | Severity | Likelihood | Business Exposure |
|-------------|----------|------------|-------------------|
Root account & MFA fragility | Critical | Medium | Organizational survivability, regulatory exposure  
Privileged access sprawl | High | High | Breach probability, audit posture  
Orphaned identities | High | Medium | Compliance failure, unauthorized access  
Over-permissioned roles | Moderate | High | Blast radius expansion  
Incident response maturity | Moderate | Medium | Impact amplification  

---

## Critical Risk

### Root Account & MFA Fragility

Failure at this level introduces non-recoverable scenarios including loss of administrative control, data exposure, and platform sovereignty.

This represents **organizational survivability risk.**

---

## High Risk

### Privileged Access Sprawl

Uncontrolled privileged access expands attack surface and materially increases breach probability.

### Orphaned Identities

Former employees retaining access represent standing compliance and security exposure.

---

## Moderate Risk

### Over-Permissioned Roles

Wildcards and inherited permissions accelerate blast radius during incidents.

### Weak Incident Response Maturity

Immature processes amplify damage when failures occur.

---

## Executive Interpretation

The environment reflects a technically capable organization entering a governance-dependent stage of maturity.

Without executive ownership and enforcement mechanisms, risk will continue to scale faster than the organization’s ability to manage it.
