##Cloud Trust & Risk Program — Cybersecurity SaaS##

#Executive Cloud Advisory | Technical Account Management Portfolio Case#

This repository documents a simulated Cloud Trust & Risk Program designed for a cybersecurity SaaS company operating a mission-critical AWS environment and serving regulated enterprise customers.

The case reflects how a Technical Account Manager or Executive Cloud Advisor partners with leadership to translate cloud conditions into business risk, governance models, and executive action.

Business Context

The customer is a cybersecurity SaaS company serving mid-to-large enterprises in regulated industries (FinTech and Healthcare).

As the company scales into enterprise contracts, leadership faces converging pressures:

• access-related security events
• audit and compliance readiness
• rising and increasingly complex cloud spend
• executive accountability for cloud risk

The cloud environment is Tier-0.
If the platform fails, customer trust fails.

This program reframes cloud operations from reactive support into an executive-governed trust system.

Program Objectives

• Establish executive visibility into cloud risk
• Harden identity and access governance
• Reduce existential and regulatory exposure
• Align cloud controls with revenue and growth
• Introduce operating rhythms for sustainable trust

Scope of Work

This program simulates the work of a Technical Account Manager embedded with a SaaS customer, including:

• Quarterly Cloud Health Reviews
• Executive risk heatmaps
• Identity posture and access governance assessment
• Billing and financial exposure framing
• Leadership-level remediation roadmaps
• Executive communication artifacts

Core Domains

• Cloud identity & access risk
• Root and MFA governance
• Privileged access containment
• Orphaned identity exposure
• Incident operating models
• Billing trust and cost visibility

Deliverables Included

• Executive Environment Snapshot
• Cloud Risk Heatmap
• Identity Posture Assessment
• Billing & Financial Exposure Review
• 30-60-90 Day Leadership Roadmap
• Governance and operating frameworks
• Executive review templates

Professional Positioning

This case represents how I work as an Executive Cloud Advisor — bridging engineering, security, finance, and leadership to operationalize cloud trust.

My focus is not only technical remediation, but building the systems, governance, and visibility structures that allow leadership to scale without hidden risk.
