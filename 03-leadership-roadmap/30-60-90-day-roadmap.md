# 30-60-90 Day Cloud Leadership Roadmap  
## Cloud Trust & Risk Program  
**Cybersecurity SaaS | AWS Environment**

---

## Purpose

This roadmap installs the operating systems required to govern identity, access, and financial exposure at enterprise scale.

The objective is not remediation.

The objective is **executive control.**

---

## First 30 Days — Authority & Stabilization

- Establish Identity Steering Committee  
- Executive root & MFA enforcement mandate  
- Identity ownership framework  
- Mandatory post-incident RCA rhythm  

Outcome: existential risk reduced, authority installed.

---

## Days 31–60 — Structural Hardening

- Privileged access rationalization  
- IAM lifecycle formalization  
- Incident response standardization  
- Billing trust alignment  

Outcome: risk stops compounding, governance becomes repeatable.

---

## Days 61–90 — Optimization & Trust Enablement

- Executive Cloud Health Dashboard  
- Formal Quarterly Cloud Health Reviews  
- Preventive control expansion  
- Cloud financial strategy activation  

Outcome: cloud becomes enterprise-scalable and governable.

---

## Executive Interpretation

This roadmap transitions cloud from an engineering system into a **business operating system.**
