# 30-60-90 Day Cloud Leadership Roadmap

### Cloud Trust & Risk Program

**Cybersecurity SaaS | AWS Environment**

## Purpose of This Roadmap

This roadmap translates the identified cloud risk domains into a phased leadership operating model.

The objective is not remediation alone.

The objective is to establish **durable executive control over identity, access, and cloud financial exposure.**

Each phase builds the structural conditions required for the next.

## Guiding Principles

• Cloud risk is business risk
• Identity is an executive control domain
• Governance precedes tooling
• Enforcement must be systemic, not voluntary
• Visibility drives accountability

## First 30 Days — Authority & Stabilization

**Strategic Objective:**
Eliminate non-recoverable failure conditions and install executive authority mechanisms.

### Leadership Operating Changes

**1. Establish an Identity Steering Committee**
A bi-weekly executive forum including the CTO, Head of Engineering, and Head of Security.

Mandate:
• approve identity policy
• own root and MFA governance
• prioritize remediation
• remove cross-team blockers

This forum becomes the decision engine for identity risk.

**2. Executive Root & MFA Enforcement Mandate**
Formal executive directive establishing a non-negotiable root and MFA standard.

Key elements:
• 14-day compliance window
• automated throttling of non-compliant identities
• exception handling owned at executive level

This shifts enforcement from technical persuasion to organizational policy.

**3. Identity Ownership Framework Deployment**
Formal designation of departmental identity owners.

Each function becomes accountable for:
• access justification
• onboarding and offboarding
• periodic access validation

This embeds identity governance into business ownership.

**4. Mandatory Post-Incident Operating Rhythm**
48-hour standardized, blame-free RCA requirement for any identity-related incident or near-miss.

Outputs:
• documented failure pathways
• control gaps
• systemic remediation actions

This establishes learning as a structural process, not an ad-hoc reaction.

**TAM Program Outcome (30 Days)**
• existential risk reduced
• enforcement authority installed
• ownership made explicit
• leadership control domains defined

## Days 31–60 — Structural Hardening

**Strategic Objective:**
Convert emergency controls into sustainable governance systems.

### Leadership Operating Changes

**1. Privileged Access Rationalization Program**
• full inventory of privileged identities
• blast-radius classification
• ownership reassignment
• access consolidation

Outcome: measurable reduction in administrative exposure.

**2. IAM Lifecycle Formalization**
• standardized access request workflows
• employment-linked offboarding triggers
• quarterly access certification cycles

Outcome: identity becomes governable at scale.

**3. Incident Response Standardization**
• identity-focused IR playbooks
• executive communication templates
• containment authority mapping

Outcome: response becomes predictable, auditable, and repeatable.

**4. Billing & Cost Trust Alignment**
• cost ownership mapping
• spend signal validation
• anomaly detection integration
• initial chargeback modeling

Outcome: cloud spend becomes interpretable by leadership.

**TAM Program Outcome (60 Days)**
• risk stops compounding
• audit posture strengthens
• response velocity improves
• financial exposure becomes visible

## Days 61–90 — Optimization & Trust Enablement

**Strategic Objective:**
Transform cloud posture into an enterprise growth enabler.

### Leadership Operating Changes

**1. Executive Cloud Health Dashboard**
Monthly executive view integrating:

• identity risk indicators
• privileged access metrics
• cost anomaly signals
• audit readiness status

Outcome: leadership gains continuous cloud visibility.

**2. Formal Quarterly Cloud Health Review Cycle**
• standardized QBR structure
• cross-functional accountability
• remediation ownership
• roadmap refresh

Outcome: cloud trust becomes an operating cadence.

**3. Preventive Control Expansion**
• least-privilege baselines
• automated compliance enforcement
• proactive anomaly detection

Outcome: risk shifts from reactive to preventive.

**4. Cloud Financial Strategy Activation**
• optimization roadmap
• RI/Savings Plans alignment
• usage-to-revenue mapping

Outcome: cloud becomes financially governable.

**TAM Program Outcome (90 Days)**
• cloud becomes enterprise-scalable
• leadership operates with clarity
• trust becomes measurable
• risk becomes strategically managed

## Executive Interpretation

This roadmap installs the governance structures required for a cybersecurity SaaS to operate as a trust provider at enterprise scale.

The primary success metric is not technical closure.

It is **leadership control.**

## Relationship to Cloud Health Reviews

Each quarterly review refreshes:

• the risk heatmap
• executive dashboards
• remediation roadmaps
• ownership accountability

This ensures the program evolves with the business.
